4 questions from weekend-class families

My child only has Chinese class once a week. How can we keep Zhuyin going between lessons?

Short, regular practice at home helps fill the gap between classes. In Lumi Bopomofo, Today's Review brings back the symbols your child hasn't mastered yet, so each session starts with the ones that need more work instead of repeating what they already know. The activities are short planet games, so a few minutes after school or before dinner is enough to keep symbols fresh until the next class.

What exactly does the app practise? Will it match what the class teaches?

Lumi Bopomofo covers all 37 Bopomofo (Zhuyin) symbols. In Feed the Friends, kids hear a sound, pick the matching symbol and feed it to a friend; in Magic Tracing they trace each symbol in stroke order with a finger; the Tone Coaster helps them hear the four tones; and the Sound Train links initial, medial and final to blend a syllable. It teaches Zhuyin only, not Pinyin, so it suits classes that use Zhuyin.

We can't read Zhuyin ourselves. Can our child practise without us?

Yes. Play is built around listening and tapping: your child hears the sound and picks the symbol, so a parent doesn't need to know Zhuyin to sit alongside. Today's Review keeps track of which symbols still need work, so you don't have to remember. The app offers 44 interface languages, including English.

Is it OK for my child to use on their own on an iPhone or iPad?

Lumi Bopomofo is completely ad-free, needs no sign-up and collects no personal data; progress is stored only on the device. The listing also notes offline play, which is handy in the car on the way to Saturday class. It is on the Australian App Store for iPhone and iPad, and on Google Play for Android.

Keeping Zhuyin going between Saturday classes? Lumi Bopomofo is free to download. Play the first symbol for free; a one-time in-app purchase unlocks all 37 symbols and every level. No subscription, no ads, no sign-up, and progress is stored only on the device.
