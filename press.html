Lumi Bopomofo: Learn Zhuyin
Press & media

Lumi Bopomofo helps young children learn all 37 Zhuyin (Bopomofo) symbols by ear: hear a sound, pick the matching symbol and feed it to a little friend.

Lumi注音星球：孩子聽發音、選出對的注音餵給小夥伴，跟著筆順描寫，坐聲調雲霄飛車分辨聲調，再用拼音小火車拼出一個音，認識全部 37 個注音符號。

Key features

  • Feed the Friends: listen and pick the right symbol
  • Magic Tracing: trace each symbol stroke by stroke
  • Tone Coaster: hear and tell apart the four tones
  • Sound Train: blend an initial, a medial and a final into one syllable
  • 37 Bopomofo friends to collect, with stars, achievement badges and hidden characters
  • Completely ad-free, collects no personal data, no sign-up; progress is stored only on the device
  • Interface in more than 40 languages
  • Try the first symbol; a one-time in-app purchase unlocks all 37 symbols and every level
