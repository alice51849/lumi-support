4 questions before a trip to Taiwan

Why would my child need Zhuyin for a visit to Taiwan?

Zhuyin, nicknamed Bopomofo, is the phonetic system children in Taiwan learn before they read many characters, and children's books there often print the symbols next to the text. Knowing the symbols lets a child sound out words in a storybook a grandparent reads with them, instead of only listening.

We have a few weeks before we fly. Is that enough time to start?

It is enough to make a start. Lumi Bopomofo is built for ages 4 to 7 and uses short planet games, so a few minutes a day fits around the end of the school term. Today's Review brings back the symbols your child hasn't mastered yet, so each session focuses on what still needs work.

Can my child keep practising on the plane or at Grandma's place?

The App Store listing notes offline play, so the games can be used on a long flight. Progress is stored only on the device, there are no ads and no sign-up, which makes it simple to hand an iPhone or iPad to a child while you travel.

Our family isn't fluent in Mandarin. Can we still help?

Yes. Play is built around listening and tapping: your child hears a sound and picks the matching symbol in Feed the Friends, traces symbols in stroke order in Magic Tracing and hears the four tones on the Tone Coaster. The app has 44 interface languages, including English, so you can follow along.

Heading to Taiwan these holidays? Lumi Bopomofo is a free download. Your child can play the first symbol for free; after that, a one-time in-app purchase unlocks all 37 symbols and every level. No subscription, no ads, no sign-up, and progress is stored only on the device.
