Printable Bopomofo flashcards — how to print and 5 ways to play

You can print a clean set of Bopomofo (Zhuyin) flashcards in a couple of minutes with our free flashcard generator: choose all 37 symbols, one group (21 initials, 3 medials or 13 finals) or any custom set, pick 4, 6 or 8 cards per A4 page, and print. This page walks through the settings and gives you five games that turn the cards into daily practice.

How to print the cards, step by step

  1. Open the Bopomofo flashcard generator on a computer or phone.
  2. Choose a quick set: All 37, Initials 21, Medials 3 or Finals 13. Or tap individual symbols to build a custom set, for example only the ones your child mixes up.
  3. Pick 4, 6 or 8 cards per page. Four large cards suit younger children and classroom display; eight smaller cards save paper for matching games.
  4. Decide whether to show the category label (initial, medial, final) on each card. Showing it helps when you sort cards into groups; hiding it makes a cleaner quiz card.
  5. Press Build flashcards, then Print. The symbols always come out once each in the official Ministry of Education order, so the same choices print the same pages every time.
  6. Cut along the lines. Card stock or laminating makes the set last through many games.

The generator does not ask for a child’s name, age or school and keeps no scores. It only prints symbols, so you will want the 37-symbol chart next to you for Pinyin, sound hints and example words.

Add trace-and-copy sheets

For pencil practice, the companion Bopomofo practice sheet generator uses the same symbol sets. Each symbol gets light example cells to trace and blank cells with crosshair guides to copy. You can choose 2, 4 or 6 example cells, 2, 4 or 6 blank cells and 4–6 symbol rows per A4 page.

The sheet does not show stroke order, so model each new symbol yourself first. Taiwan’s Ministry of Education publishes the official stroke order (see the source link at the bottom of this page). Keep sessions short: three or four symbols at a time is plenty for a young child.

Five games to play with Bopomofo flashcards

1. Sound hunt

Hide 6–10 cards around a room. When your child finds one, they say its sound before putting it in the basket. For an extra challenge, they must also think of a word starting with that sound (ㄇ → 媽, ㄍ → 狗).

2. Snap with sounds

Print two copies of a small set. Take turns flipping cards; when two match, the first person to say the sound (not just “snap!”) keeps the pair. This rewards recall, not reflex.

3. Build-a-syllable train

Lay cards in a row like train cars: initial, medial, final, then a tone card. ㄏ + ㄨ + ㄚ = 花 huā. Swap one card and read the new syllable. Swapping ㄏ for ㄍ turns 花 into 瓜 (melon).

4. Family sort

Mix the cards and sort them into lip sounds (ㄅㄆㄇㄈ), tongue-tip sounds (ㄉㄊㄋㄌ), back-of-mouth sounds (ㄍㄎㄏ), flat-tongue (ㄐㄑㄒ), curled-tongue (ㄓㄔㄕㄖ), straight-tongue (ㄗㄘㄙ), medials and finals. Sorting by mouth position helps children feel the difference, especially between ㄓ/ㄗ and ㄕ/ㄙ.

5. Missing card

Lay out five cards in the official order (for example ㄅㄆㄇㄈㄉ). Your child closes their eyes while you remove one. Which one is gone? Increase to eight or ten cards as they get comfortable.

Frequently asked questions

Are the printable flashcards free?

Yes. The flashcard and practice sheet generators are free web tools with no account; you print directly from your browser.

Do the flashcards include Pinyin or pictures?

No. The cards show the official symbols only (optionally with the category label). Use the chart for Pinyin, sound hints and example words.

What paper size do they use?

The pages are laid out for A4. On US Letter paper, choose “fit to page” in your print dialog.

Which order are the cards printed in?

Selected symbols always print once each in the official Taiwan Ministry of Education order, never in a random shuffle, so a teacher and parents can print identical sets.
