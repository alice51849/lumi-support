4 questions to decide before you download

Does Lumi Bopomofo teach Pinyin too?

No. Lumi Bopomofo teaches Zhuyin (Bopomofo) only: the 37 symbols, the four tones and blending symbols into syllables. If your child needs to learn Pinyin, a Pinyin-focused tool will suit you better.

How do we decide whether our child should start with Zhuyin or Pinyin?

Both are ways to show how Mandarin is pronounced. Pinyin uses Latin letters and is the system used in mainland China and many Chinese programs; Zhuyin uses its own symbols and is what children in Taiwan learn, so many books from Taiwan print Zhuyin next to traditional characters. A simple rule of thumb: pick the one your child's class, books and family already use.

My child's Chinese class uses Pinyin. Is a Zhuyin app still worth it?

It depends on your family. If you read storybooks from Taiwan with Zhuyin beside the characters, or relatives in Taiwan read with your child, Zhuyin can be a useful extra at home. If Pinyin is the only system your child meets in class and at home, it usually makes sense to focus on Pinyin first.

If we choose Zhuyin, what does the app actually practise?

Kids hear a sound, pick the matching symbol and feed it to a friend; trace each symbol in stroke order with Magic Tracing; ride the Tone Coaster to hear the four tones; and link initial, medial and final on the Sound Train to blend a syllable.
