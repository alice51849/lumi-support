5 questions before you download

My child understands Mandarin at home but can't read it. How does Lumi Bopomofo connect sounds to symbols?

It starts with listening. Kids learn all 37 Bopomofo (Zhuyin) symbols by ear: they hear a sound, pick the matching symbol and feed it to a friend. That makes the sounds your child already knows from home the starting point for reading the symbols.

Does my child practice writing the symbols, or only tapping?

Both. In Magic Tracing, your child traces each symbol with a finger, stroke by stroke, in stroke order. The listening game, Feed the Friends, covers recognition, so writing and recognizing are practiced side by side.

Tones are the hardest part for us. How does the app handle them?

Tones get their own game, the Tone Coaster. Your child watches the cart ride and hears the tones, which turns the four tones into something they can see as well as hear.

Can it help my child go from single symbols to whole syllables?

Yes. On the Sound Train, your child links an initial, a medial and a final to blend a syllable. This is the step from knowing individual symbols to sounding out a real syllable.

We only practice on weekends and my child forgets symbols in between. Is there review?

Today's Review brings back the symbols your child hasn't mastered yet, so practice time goes to the ones that still need work. To keep kids coming back, there are 37 Bopomofo friends to collect, stars and achievement badges to earn, and hidden characters to discover.

Getting started on iPhone The first symbol is open from the start; all 37 symbols and every level unlock with a one-time in-app purchase. Completely ad-free, no sign-up, no personal data collected, and progress is stored only on the device.
