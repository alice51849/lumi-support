A 10-minute writing routine for ages 4 to 7

  1. Hear it first. Play Feed the Friends so your child knows which sound the symbol stands for.
  2. Trace with a finger. In Magic Tracing, trace the same symbol stroke by stroke, two or three times.
  3. Write it in the air, then on paper. Say each stroke out loud ("top first, then down") while your child writes it big.
  4. End with Today's Review. It brings back the symbols your child hasn't mastered yet, so you don't have to keep a list.

4 questions before you download

Does stroke order really matter for Bopomofo?

Yes, for neat and readable handwriting. Bopomofo symbols are built from the same kinds of strokes as Chinese characters, and children in Taiwan learn them in a fixed order. A child who draws each symbol in a different way every time tends to write shapes that teachers and grandparents struggle to read, and the habit carries over to characters later.

What are the basic stroke-order rules?

Most symbols follow the general rules of Chinese handwriting: top before bottom, left before right, and a horizontal stroke before a vertical one that crosses it. These are general guidelines; the safest way is to copy the order shown stroke by stroke rather than guess.
