4 questions about Zhuyin and Pinyin

What is the difference between Zhuyin and Pinyin?

Both show how Mandarin is pronounced. Pinyin uses the Latin alphabet. Zhuyin, nicknamed Bopomofo after its first symbols ㄅ ㄆ ㄇ ㄈ, uses 37 symbols of its own and is the system children in Taiwan learn first. Taiwanese children's books often print Zhuyin next to the characters.

How do I know which one my child should practise?

Follow whatever your child's teacher or family uses. Classes differ, so it is worth checking the worksheets your child brings home: Latin letters with tone marks mean Pinyin, while small symbols such as ㄅ ㄆ ㄇ printed beside characters mean Zhuyin. If relatives in Taiwan read with your child, Zhuyin is the one they will recognise.

Does Lumi Bopomofo teach Pinyin as well?

No. Lumi Bopomofo teaches Zhuyin only. It covers all 37 symbols: in Feed the Friends kids hear a sound and pick the matching symbol, Magic Tracing practises stroke order, the Tone Coaster trains the four tones, and the Sound Train blends initial, medial and final into a syllable.
