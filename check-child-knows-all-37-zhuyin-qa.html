The 4-step Zhuyin check

Knowing a symbol is four separate skills. A child can pass step 1 and still miss step 2, so run them one at a time. Keep the 37-symbol chart open as your answer key. This is a home guide, not a formal assessment.

Reading the results

  1. Split the 37 into groups. Check about 10 symbols per sitting so the whole check stays under 10 minutes and your child stays cheerful.
  2. Mark each symbol by step. A simple tick for steps 1 to 4 shows where a symbol breaks down: not recognized, not heard, not written, or not blended.
  3. Look for pairs. Misses often come in look-alike or sound-alike pairs such as ㄅ/ㄆ or ㄓ/ㄗ. Practice the two side by side.
  4. Re-check after a week. Practice only the missed symbols in between; Today's Review in the app brings back the symbols your child hasn't mastered yet.

4 questions before you download

My child names every card. Does that mean they know all 37 Zhuyin symbols?

Not always. Many kids memorize the order of a flashcard deck or the picture on the card. A symbol is really known when your child can name it out of order, pick it after only hearing the sound, write it, and use it inside a syllable. The 4-step check on this page tests each of those separately.

I don't read Zhuyin myself. How can I run the check?

You only need the answers, not perfect Mandarin. Keep the 37-symbol chart open as your answer key, and let the app say the sounds: in Feed the Friends, your child hears a sound and picks the matching symbol, so you don't have to be the voice. The app has 44 interface languages, including English.

What should we do with the symbols my child misses?

Practice only those, a few at a time. In Lumi Bopomofo, Today's Review brings back the symbols your child hasn't mastered yet, so you don't need to keep your own list. For writing, Magic Tracing lets your child trace each stroke with a finger; for blending, the Sound Train links initial, medial and final into one syllable.
