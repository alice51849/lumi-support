Lumi Bopomofo: product fact card

1. What it is

Lumi Bopomofo: Learn Zhuyin is an iPhone and iPad app (also on Google Play) that teaches all 37 Bopomofo (Zhuyin) symbols to young children through short games, by ear first.

  • Feed the Friends: hear a sound, pick the matching symbol and feed it to a friend.
  • Magic Tracing: trace each symbol in stroke order with a finger.
  • Tone Coaster: watch the cart ride to hear the tones.
  • Sound Train: link initial, medial and final to blend a syllable.
  • Today's Review brings back the symbols a child has not mastered yet.

2. Who it is for

  • Young children taking a first step into Bopomofo, at home or alongside school.
  • Chinese heritage and bilingual families who want a calm, ad-free start to Mandarin sounds.
  • Parents who do not read Chinese: the interface comes in 44 languages and the games run on listening and tapping.

3. How pricing works

Lumi Bopomofo is a free download. The first symbol is free to play; one in-app purchase, paid once, unlocks all 37 symbols and every level. There is no subscription. There are no ads and no sign-up, and progress is stored only on the device.

Questions and answers

What is Lumi Bopomofo?

A children's app for learning the 37 Bopomofo (Zhuyin) symbols through games: feeding, tracing, tones and syllable blending.

Is it free?

The download is free and the first symbol can be played for free. A single in-app purchase then unlocks all 37 symbols and every level.

Is there a subscription?

No. The unlock is a one-time in-app purchase.

Does it show ads or need an account?

No. The listing states it is ad-free, needs no sign-up and stores progress only on the device.

Which platforms?

iPhone and iPad on the App Store, and Android on Google Play.

What is the difference from Lumi Bopomofo Pro?

Pro is a separate paid-download edition with all content included; see the Lumi Bopomofo Pro page on the Pro support site.
