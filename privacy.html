01 What we store

The app stores your child's learning progress (unlocked characters, stars, tones heard, and settings) only on the device. It is never transmitted to us or any third party, and is removed when you delete the app. App 僅在裝置本機儲存孩子的學習進度（已解鎖的字、星星、聽過的聲調與設定）。此資料不會傳送給我們或任何第三方，並在你刪除 App 時一併移除。

02 Backup & Restore

The optional Export / Import feature saves the progress file to your own device / Files app, in a location you choose. We never receive or store this file. 選用的「匯出 / 匯入」功能會將進度檔存到你自己的裝置／檔案 App，位置由你決定，我們不會收到或保存該檔。

03 Purchases

The free edition offers an optional one-time "full unlock" in-app purchase; on iPhone and iPad the transaction is handled by the Apple App Store, and on Android it is handled by Google Play. We never receive or store your payment information, card number, Apple Account or Google Account. The Pro edition contains no in-app purchases. 免費版提供選用的一次性「完整解鎖」內購，在 iPhone 與 iPad 上由 Apple App Store 處理交易，在 Android 上則由 Google Play 處理。我們不會收到或儲存你的付款資訊、信用卡號、Apple 帳號或 Google 帳號。Pro 版本身不含任何內購。

04 Children's privacy

The app is designed for children (around ages 4–7). Because it collects no personal information at all, it complies with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU GDPR-K. There is no behavioral advertising and no third-party data sharing. 本 App 為兒童（約 4–7 歲）設計。因完全不蒐集個人資訊，符合美國 COPPA 與歐盟 GDPR-K，沒有行為廣告，也沒有任何第三方資料分享。

05 Permissions

The app requests no sensitive permissions (no camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos, or notifications). Audio playback and text-to-speech run entirely on the device. The same is true of the Android edition: it never asks you to grant a runtime permission, and it uses internet access only so that Google Play can process a purchase or show the optional rating prompt, plus vibration for gentle haptic feedback. App 不要求相機、麥克風、定位、聯絡人、照片或通知等任何敏感權限；音訊播放與語音合成皆於裝置本機運作。Android 版亦同：從不要求你授予任何執行階段權限，網路連線僅供 Google Play 處理購買與顯示選用的評分提示，另使用震動提供輕微的觸覺回饋。

For parents: children's privacy at a glance 給家長：兒童隱私重點

This is a summary of the policy above. It adds no new practice and does not change it. 以下是上方政策的重點整理，沒有新增或變更任何做法。

What information is collected from my child?

None. The app does not ask for, collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information from children or parents. There are no accounts and no sign-in. Learning progress and settings, and anything your child creates or enters in the app, stay only on your device and are removed when you delete the app. 沒有。App 不會向孩子或家長要求、蒐集、傳送、販售或分享任何個人資訊；沒有帳號、不需登入。學習進度、設定，以及孩子在 App 內建立或輸入的內容，都只存在你的裝置上，刪除 App 時一併移除。

Advertising and tracking

No advertising of any kind, no behavioral advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs that collect data. The app does not track your child across other companies' apps or websites. 沒有任何廣告、沒有行為式廣告、沒有數據分析、沒有追蹤，也沒有會蒐集資料的第三方 SDK；App 不會跨其他公司的 App 或網站追蹤孩子。

COPPA and GDPR-K

Because no personal information is collected from children, no parental consent for data collection is needed, nothing is disclosed to third parties, and the app is designed to comply with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the children's provisions of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR-K). Purchases and links that lead outside the app are meant for parents and are handled as described above. 由於不向兒童蒐集任何個人資訊，因此不需要為資料蒐集取得家長同意，也沒有任何資料揭露給第三方；本 App 依美國《兒童線上隱私保護法》（COPPA）與歐盟 GDPR 兒童條款（GDPR-K）設計。購買與離開 App 的連結是給家長使用，處理方式如上方政策所述。

Your rights as a parent

We hold no personal information about you or your child, so there is nothing on our side to review, export or delete. You can erase everything the app stored at any time by deleting the app from the device. If you have any question or request about your child's privacy, contact us and we will reply. 我們沒有保存任何關於你或孩子的個人資訊，因此在我們這端沒有可供查閱、匯出或刪除的資料。你隨時可以從裝置刪除 App，以清除 App 儲存的所有內容。若對孩子的隱私有任何問題或要求，歡迎與我們聯絡，我們會回覆。
