Privacy

Lumi Bopomofo · Privacy

Data and privacy

Learning progress and settings stay on the device. There are no third-party ads, analytics or tracking, and no child account is required.

Accounts, ads and tracking

No account is required. The app contains no third-party advertising, cross-app tracking or third-party analytics.

Purchase

The remaining visible learning content opens through an optional one-time purchase handled by Apple. A parent or guardian should use the purchase and external-link controls.

Deletion and recovery

Delete records from inside the app when that control is available. Deleting the app removes its local container; Apple-managed backups remain governed by your device settings.
