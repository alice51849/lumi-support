A 5-minute routine for one tricky sound

  1. Pick one row from the table, not all of them. One sound pair a day is plenty for ages 4 to 7.
  2. Listen twice, then say it once. Tap the speaker, listen together, and only then ask your child to say it.
  3. Hear the difference before saying it. In Feed the Friends, your child hears a sound and picks the matching symbol, so hearing comes before speaking.
  4. Finish with Today's Review. It brings back the symbols your child hasn't mastered yet, so you don't have to keep a list.

4 questions before you download

Which Zhuyin sounds do English-speaking kids usually find hardest?

The usual trouble spots are the curled-tongue sounds ㄓ ㄔ ㄕ ㄖ versus the flat-tongue ㄗ ㄘ ㄙ, the front sounds ㄐ ㄑ ㄒ, the vowel ㄩ (ü), which has no English match, and the tones, especially the low dipping third tone. These are general patterns for English speakers, not a diagnosis of any one child.

Why does my child say the sound right alone but wrong inside a word?

A single symbol and a full syllable are two different skills. A child can know ㄓ and ㄨ separately and still blur them when blending. In Lumi Bopomofo, the Sound Train links initial, medial and final into one syllable, so blending gets its own practice.

I don't speak Mandarin well. How can I tell if my child's sound is right?

You don't have to be the model. Each game lets your child listen to the sound again, and Feed the Friends asks your child to hear a sound and pick the matching symbol, so listening carefully comes first. The app has 44 interface languages, including English, so you can follow along.
